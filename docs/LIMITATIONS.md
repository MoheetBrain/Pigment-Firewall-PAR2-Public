# Limitations and historical defects

No new candidate has established binding, antagonism, pigmentation reduction or
safety. Structural scores cannot resolve pathway bias. The selective-PAR2
hypothesis for melanocore uptake is itself unverified. The G12/13–RhoA functional
counter-screen is essential.

The September 5 CSV is historical: RX05505 has a stereo provenance mismatch;
RX00387 has a constitutional provenance mismatch. Earlier tautomer processing
also affected thousands of designs, including 207 finalists docked as the wrong
stereoisomer. Preserve that history when studying the older panel; do not treat
it as a clean material-order list. Earlier real-compound rows had false de novo
labels. The later sourceable panel was prepared through the corrected v3 route.

The interaction receptor had no charged lysines under its defective protonation,
so salt bridges were underdetected. Pocket-filling can yield high IFP recovery;
the missing decoy null prevents calibration of that feature. Allosteric hotspot
support is weaker. The sourceability panel lacks completed co-folding, active-state
and enrichment deliverables. Its active-state warning field is an export fallback,
not measured counter-screen evidence. There is no basis for claiming a passed
functional or active-state hard gate.

Vendor listings were historical, not verified stock or price. The approval tag
was overbroad (including clinical-stage resatorvid). Similarity-based sourceability
failed for the generated chemistry; most later finalists are repurposing choices.
Original inherited comments claiming stronger validation are subordinate to these
explicit limitations. No numerical or structural field was silently corrected.

The saved panel uses four families (70% nominal weight): F1, F2, F4 and F6.
F3 and F5 are absent. The available `seed_tanimoto` input is not recognized by
F5's inherited input-name mapping, so resemblance to de novo seeds does not
contribute through that family. Similarity is reported as provenance, not as
an implemented F5 contribution. The published scores and order remain unchanged.
