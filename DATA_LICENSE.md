## ZINC20

The [official ZINC20 2D tranche terms](https://cache.docking.org/2D/) permit sharing
search and screening results while requiring written permission to redistribute
major portions. This release includes a small research-result panel, not tranche
files, bulk libraries or a ZINC database mirror. The broader AWS ZINC3D entry has
more restrictive wording; this release contains no such 3D model archive.
Acknowledge the ZINC database and its authors; see
[ZINC20, J. Chem. Inf. Model. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.0c00675).
Do not treat ZINC input data as CC BY or MIT merely because our scores use those licences.

## PubChem

Limited chemical identifiers, structures and matching/sourceability facts are
retained as provenance for the original research results; supplier prose,
product descriptions, API responses and bulk catalogues are excluded. PubChem
and the contributing data sources retain their rights. NCBI imposes no added
restriction on molecular-data distribution but cannot convey contributors'
rights: read its [usage policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/).
NCBI does not endorse this work or any compound. No accuracy, completeness,
fitness or safety warranty is provided.

## Other inputs

The full campaign used natural-product and repurposing libraries, but none of
those bulk datasets is redistributed. Obtain required inputs from their lawful
sources and observe source-specific terms. Enamine catalogues and their bulk or
derived exports are excluded; current terms do not establish redistribution
permission. The selected final panels were traced to original generated designs
and PubChem/ZINC-based repurposing/search outputs, rather than an Enamine catalogue export.

Protein coordinates are obtained separately from the wwPDB archive (CC0 1.0);
cite each deposition and its authors. Papers and website prose are linked rather
than redistributed. Scientific references do not imply institutional endorsement.
RDKit, GNINA, ProLIF, Boltz and other dependencies are not relicensed by this repository.
